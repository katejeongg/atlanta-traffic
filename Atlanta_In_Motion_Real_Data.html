The takeaway · System 2 in practice

Don't just ask “how many?” Ask “out of how many?”

The availability heuristic helps explain why vivid, frequently reported crashes can dominate our impressions. System 1 makes the quick comparison; System 2 checks the denominator, geography, severity definition, and matching assumptions. Raw totals reveal the burden of crashes, while exposure-adjusted rates address a different question. Neither alone predicts the safety of your next trip.

Driving in Atlanta: Focus on specific collision contexts, follow distances, lane changes, attention, and speed appropriate to conditions. This historical dataset cannot recommend an individually safest highway or departure time.

Data provenance. Crash records: GDOT 2021–2025 user-supplied GPAS-enriched file (373,976 rows); GPAS official road names; GDOT Traffic GeoDatabase and Road Inventory; historical TADA AADT. Corridor exposure estimates use 2025 road geometries and matching of reported road names to primary interstate RouteIDs, excluding ramp-coded crashes. For the four freeway rates, unmatched mainline crash records and 2025 roadway geometry are sources of uncertainty; these are class-project exploratory estimates, not official transportation safety rankings. Hourly charts use crash counts only.
