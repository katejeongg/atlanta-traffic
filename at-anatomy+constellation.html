Source: master_dataset_no_tmas.csv, filtered to 2021–2023. The perimeter collision CSV has the same 373,976 crash rows in its 23 shared fields, so records are not counted twice. Corridor comparisons use the master’s gpas_verified_corridor assignments; unresolved matches are not assigned. TMAS.csv contributes coverage context only: its traffic-station IDs do not match crash candidate-station IDs, so vehicle counts are not joined to corridors and no exposure-adjusted rate is implied. Counts and K/A shares are descriptive crash summaries.
